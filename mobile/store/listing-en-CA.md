## Full description (App Store and Google Play, max 4000)

Who's right? Settle it.

Judge is a small, opinionated referee for everyday arguments. Tell it what you're fighting about and it will say who's right. Or, just as handy, why nobody is.

Dishes, plans, money, movies, whether pineapple belongs on pizza: write down both sides and let three AI judges rule.

HOW IT WORKS
- You describe the argument, including the other side the way they'd put it.
- Names are swapped for Party A and Party B before judging, so the judges never know who's who.
- Three independent AI judges each make the call on their own. On a close call, more judges are called in.
- You get a verdict and a confidence score. If the judges disagree, the score drops and tells you.
- A short "Why" says in two or three plain sentences what actually decided it.
- Every verdict lists the facts it leans on, so you can check them yourself.

HONEST VERDICTS
Not every fight has a winner. A verdict can be a clear win, a split decision, "it depends on your values", or "same word, different meanings". If you still want a winner, you get a forced ruling, clearly labelled as one.

FEATURES
- Three independent AI judges and an honest confidence score
- Names swapped out before judging
- Facts listed so you can check them
- Your past verdicts on any device you sign in on, with what you wrote stored encrypted
- Hear both sides: the other person tells their side through a private link, and neither of you sees the other's text until the verdict
- Share a verdict with a link, showing only the names you choose (or none)
- Remove a verdict, or your whole account, any time
- Available in 45 languages

EVERY VERDICT
Every verdict gets the full treatment:
- Facts checked on the web, with sources shown on your verdict
- The full panel of AI judges
- Up to two quick questions when a key fact is missing
- Screenshot evidence, such as a chat or a receipt: we read the text and never keep the image
- Add context after a verdict and have it re-judged (uses one verdict)
Your first verdict is free with a new account. After that, get verdicts in packs, or with the monthly plan, which includes 35 verdicts a month. Two-sided cases use one verdict too.

YOUR PRIVACY
- Sign in with Apple, Google or an emailed code.
- Your text goes to an outside AI service so names can be swapped for Party A and Party B. Only that anonymized version is judged.
- What you write, names included, is stored encrypted so you can look at it again. You can have it removed any time.
- No ad trackers, and we never sell your data.
- Details: https://okjudge.com/privacy

GOOD TO KNOW
AI judges can get facts wrong, and the way you tell the story shapes the result. Judge is for fun and for thinking a bit more clearly about everyday spats. It is not legal, medical or financial advice. For anything serious, talk to a qualified human.

Questions or requests about your data: https://okjudge.com
