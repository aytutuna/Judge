# App Review notes (English)

Judge is an app for settling everyday arguments. The user describes an argument and three AI judges each give a verdict, with a confidence score.

## How to test

1. Open the app. Sign in is required (Apple, Google or an emailed code).
2. Use the reviewer test account:
   - Email: <REVIEW_EMAIL>
   - Code: <REVIEW_CODE>

   Choose the email option, enter the email, then enter the code.
3. Describe an argument (for example: "Is a hot dog a sandwich?") and ask for a verdict. A verdict takes a short while to arrive.

## Outside AI service and consent

The text the user writes is sent to an outside AI service so names can be swapped for Party A and Party B and the argument judged. The sign-in screen tells the user this before they continue, and signing in is how they agree. Text is stored encrypted and the user can remove it. No ads, no tracking, no data sold.

## Account deletion

Settings > Account > Delete account. This removes the account, sign-in details, verdicts and the user's side of any cases.

## Purchases

Premium is sold through in-app purchase: two consumable credit packs (`pack_1`, `pack_10`) and an auto-renewable monthly subscription (`monthly`) that includes 35 premium verdicts a month. The sandbox works for testing: sign in with the reviewer account, open Settings > Pricing, and buy with a sandbox account. The credits or the plan show up within a few seconds. Restore purchases is under Settings > Account.

## Two-sided cases

Two-sided cases are free for any signed-in user, no purchase needed. Pick "Both sides" on the home screen, create the invite, then open the link in a browser to answer as the other person.

## Other

- Not legal, medical or financial advice. The app says so.
- User text is private unless the user shares a verdict link. There is no feed, comments or messaging.
- Support: https://okjudge.com. Privacy policy: https://okjudge.com/privacy
